Payment

Payments are processed by Stripe through ExtensionPay (extensionpay.com). We never see or store your card details. Your purchase is linked to the email you pay with, which lets you restore Pro on another computer or after reinstalling, from the “Restore Pro by email” link in the extension.

If Athbee Bookmark Pro isn't right for you, email eduardorosa.drd@gmail.com within 14 days of purchase with the email you paid with, and you'll get a full refund, no questions asked. Refunds go back to the original payment method through Stripe, usually within 5–10 business days depending on your bank. Once refunded, Pro features turn off; the free features and all your data stay as they are.

Your data

Your bookmarks, history, tags and sessions stay in your browser. See the privacy policy for exactly what is accessed and when anything leaves your computer.

Availability and changes

We work to keep Athbee Bookmark working on current versions of Chrome, Brave and Edge, but it is provided “as is”, without warranties.
